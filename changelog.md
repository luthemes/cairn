# Change Log

## 1.0.0
- Initial release.
- Block theme built with theme.json and the Site Editor, with no framework or build step.
- Two-column layout: posts on the left and an editable sidebar template part on the right.
- Two-column grid of featured images on the blog, archive and search pages.
- Bundled Inter font, with nothing loaded from other sites.
- Templates for posts, pages, archives, search and 404.
- Styled threaded comments and a sidebar with About, Search, Categories and Recent posts.
